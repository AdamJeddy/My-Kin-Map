# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The initial audience is a family organizer creating their first family tree. The owner wants the product to serve a wider public over time; the initial audience is a recommendation to validate.

## Product Purpose

Help people bring their family story together by recording people, relationships and memories, and exploring how they connect.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing app stores family data in the browser, supports JSON backup, and works on mobile and desktop. Preserve existing data and working actions. Device-local storage and a permanently free service are not fixed long-term product commitments; optional sync and paid services may be considered later. No account, sharing, or sync is currently implemented. Existing relationship-model limitations are recorded in `docs/revival-review/IMPLEMENTATION-STATUS.md`.

## Product Principles

- Help a beginner build a meaningful tree without needing genealogy expertise.
- Treat family information carefully and make recovery and ownership understandable.
- Make people and relationships easy to find on mobile and desktop.
- Validate product choices with real users; do not imply future features already exist.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a fictional sample family and current working app. The review and remaining risks are in `docs/revival-review/`.
