# Product UI and UX audit

15 November 2026. Scope: first run, desktop tree/navigation, mobile family view, mobile tree, people search and settings, using the bundled fictional sample. Captures are from the current local browser audit; the before and after screenshots are linked below. This is a focused product audit, not a complete accessibility or security assessment.

## Steps and findings

1. **First run — workable, needs clearer demo boundaries.** The [desktop empty state](ui-audit/desktop-start-before.jpg) offers a first person and sample family. Loading sample records adds them to the same browser database; make the demo and cleanup consequences clearer in a future change, particularly for existing data.
2. **Desktop tree and navigation — improved.** The [original desktop capture](ui-audit/desktop-before.jpg) shows a 256px expanded sidebar with prominent branding and spacing. The [revised view](ui-audit/desktop-after.jpg) uses a 224px sidebar, smaller brand mark and tighter vertical rhythm; it retains readable labels and collapse/expand. The sample tree's fit view still makes names small, and its minimap obscures some canvas space. Larger or complex trees need further layout and readability testing.
3. **Mobile family exploration — improved.** In the [original view](ui-audit/mobile-before.jpg), four bottom-bar actions place Add off center, and the profile's notes and management controls precede relationships. The [revised view](ui-audit/mobile-after.jpg) centers Add between Tree and Settings, exposes a labeled Find people link above the profile, and places partners and children before notes and family-group management. Profile relationship rows keep at least 44px height. Search, quick-add, family group management, switching views and following a relative were exercised after the change.
4. **Mobile tree and people — functional, readability needs work.** The [mobile tree](ui-audit/mobile-tree-before.jpg) keeps graph controls and access to people search, but names become small for a 17-person family. The [people directory](ui-audit/mobile-people-before.jpg) provides a labeled search and tappable rows. The directory remains accessible through Find people from Family and Find a person from Tree after removing it from the bottom bar.
5. **Settings and recovery — improved.** The [original settings](ui-audit/mobile-settings-before.jpg) put a lengthy About panel before theme and recovery actions. The [revised settings](ui-audit/mobile-settings-after.jpg) move About lower, bringing backup controls into the initial mobile view. Export was checked after the change; the deletion confirmation remains separate.

## Accessibility and verification limits

- Checked visible controls and browser accessibility names, 390px and 320px mobile layouts, light and dark themes, desktop navigation collapse/expand, relative selection, add-person entry, quick-add save, group manager, search and settings export. The centered button measured 44px wide and within a fraction of a pixel of the viewport center at 390px.
- The keyboard focus system exists, but a complete keyboard-only journey, screen-reader use, formal contrast measurement, device text scaling and real touch/physical-device checks were not completed. Screenshots and browser emulation do not establish accessibility conformance.
- The 17-person tree can still be hard to read at overview scale on mobile and desktop. Explore focused-tree navigation, responsive zoom and more realistic complex-family fixtures rather than assuming a single fit setting solves it.
- Sample data currently shares the live database, and product copy around sample loading deserves a separate data-safety review. Existing dependency advisory, legacy GEDCOM lint and restore compatibility follow-ups remain in [HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md).
