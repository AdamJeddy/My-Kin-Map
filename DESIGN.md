# Design direction

My Kin Map is an operating interface for collecting and exploring family connections. Its visual direction is a quiet family archive: warm surfaces, forest-green navigation, clear human-scale typography, and family cards that keep the person as the focus. Standard controls and familiar navigation take precedence over decorative metaphors.

The tree is a working canvas. Its cards, relationship lines, zoom controls and mobile person list must remain legible and interactive. The dark theme is a supported variation. Keep private data, existing names and relationships, and recovery actions intact.
